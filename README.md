# Financial Transactions & Fraud Detection Dashboard

An interactive 3-page **Power BI** analytics dashboard analyzing **50,000 financial transactions** totaling **3.18M** across **1,000 customers**[cite: 6, 8]. The report provides a complete view of transaction performance, customer demographics, and fraud detection patterns[cite: 6, 7, 8].

---

## Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Transactions Overview
* **Core KPIs:** Tracked **3.18M** in total transaction amount across **50.00K** transactions, with an average ticket of **63.69**, **158.09K** in total discounts, and an average fraud rate of **0.01**[cite: 8].
* **Spending Breakdown:** **Travel** led spending by category, followed by **Home** and **Shopping (Net)**[cite: 8].
* **Payment Methods & Channels:** Analyzed spending across **Credit Card**, **Debit Card**, and **Digital Wallet**, as well as **In-Store** vs. **Online** channels, with interactive filters for transaction status (`approved`, `declined`, `refunded`) and month[cite: 8].

### 2. Customer Analytics
* **Customer Base:** Analyzed **1,000 customers** generating **3.18M** in total amount with an average ticket of **63.69**[cite: 6].
* **Demographics & Loyalty:** Evaluated spending across loyalty tiers (**Platinum**, **Bronze**, **Silver**, **Gold**), customer states (led by **TX** and **CA**), and gender distribution[cite: 6].
* **Top Customers:** Highlighted top spenders along with their state, loyalty tier, total amount, and transaction count[cite: 6].

### 3. Fraud Detection & Risk Analysis
* **Fraud Metrics:** Identified **310 flagged transactions** out of **50.00K** total transactions (**0.01** average fraud rate)[cite: 7].
* **Risk Patterns:** Analyzed flagged transactions by category (led by **shopping_net**), entry mode (highest in **online**), and device type (peaking on **pos_terminal**)[cite: 7].
* **Merchant & Monthly Monitoring:** Tracked monthly fraud trends and highlighted merchants with the highest number of flagged transactions[cite: 7].
